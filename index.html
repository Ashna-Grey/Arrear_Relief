What do these terms mean?

Receipt year

The financial year in which the arrears were actually paid into your account – not the year they were earned.

Arrears

Salary or DA that was due to you in earlier years but paid to you late, in a lump sum.

Taxable income (not gross salary)

What is left of your income after standard deduction and other allowed deductions. Use the figure on which your tax is calculated.

Tax payable

Total tax including applicable rebate and cess. Do not subtract TDS already deducted – we compare actual tax, not what was paid.

Extra tax in receipt year

How much more tax you pay this year just because the arrears were added (tax after arrears minus tax before arrears).

Amount belonging to the earlier year

The part of your arrears that was really due in that year. It should come from your arrears statement. All parts must add up to the total arrears.

Tax without / with that year's arrear

Your actual tax for that earlier year as it was, and what it would have been if that year's share of arrears had been included.

Extra tax if spread over earlier years

The total of extra tax each earlier year would have had if the arrears had been received on time.

Estimated relief

How much of this year's extra tax you may save because the arrears are spread back over earlier years. It is never below zero.

Net extra tax after relief

The extra tax you still pay in the receipt year after the relief is deducted.

Good to know

  • How it works: Relief = (extra tax in receipt year) − (extra tax in earlier years). If the earlier years would have cost the same or more, relief is ₹0.
  • Include rebate and cess in all tax figures. Exclude TDS.
  • You must calculate your tax figures yourself (or with your DDO / tax adviser) using the correct slab rates for each year. This tool does the comparison for you.
  • Your data is never uploaded. Everything is calculated in your browser.

Important: This is an indicative comparison only. It does not confirm your eligibility for relief or the legally correct amount. Confirm the applicable provision, year-wise computations and Form 39 / documentation with your DDO or a tax adviser before filing.
